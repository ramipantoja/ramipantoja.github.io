About

Rasp Films makes distinctive, stylised genre films with emerging filmmakers.

Founded by Rami Sarras Pantoja, a British producer of Chilean and Palestinian heritage, Rasp backs filmmakers whose stories make the everyday feel dark, funny and unexpected.

Rami produced the BAFTA-nominated Lucky Break and co-produced the BIFA-nominated Housewarming. His post production work spans film and television for Netflix, Apple, Disney and Warner Bros. He is a BAFTA voting member and has served on its short film jury.
